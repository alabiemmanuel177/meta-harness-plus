## Methodology

Per-repo pace is derived from dev_100 rerank checkpoint mtimes (workers=1, sequential). The first instance is excluded (its delta would include embedder warmup). Inter-instance gaps above 30 minutes are clipped, since they reflect a power outage / restart, not actual pace.

Reranker cost is calibrated from the actual dev_100 checkpoint totals (commits 12a/12b):
  - deepseek-chat: $0.0024/instance (observed across two 100-instance runs, $0.24 each).
  - claude-sonnet-4-5: $0.0400/instance (observed across two 100-instance runs, $4.00 each).
Rerank step latency averaged 14s for DeepSeek and 25s for Sonnet on dev_100; the Sonnet projection adds the per-instance latency delta on top of the dev_100-derived per-repo pace (which itself was measured under DeepSeek).
